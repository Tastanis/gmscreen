# Character builder rules catalog

The human-readable rules.json catalog adapts the Draw Steel Heroes game data organized by Andy Aiken and the Forge Steel contributors. Names, keywords and ranges for the Elementalist were checked against the local Heroes v1.01b rules, including Practical Magic and Muse of Fire. The Null uses the same revision for ability groups and traditions. Its action cards and Discipline Mastery thresholds follow the local Heroes v1.01b text. Censor order, domain and kit records adapt the same revision. Censor cards, domain features, kit bonuses and kit signatures use the local Heroes v1.01b chapters. Conduit uses the pinned class structure and the local Conduit chapter for its advancement, paired domains, and ability cards. Fury uses the pinned aspect structure and the local Fury chapter for shared and aspect abilities, Growing Ferocity tables and Stormwight kits. Deity and saint portfolios follow the chapter 14 tables. The catalog is distributed under GPL-3.0. Its editable JSON is the preferred form for modifications here.
