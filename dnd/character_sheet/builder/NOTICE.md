# Character builder rules catalog

The human-readable rules.json catalog adapts the Draw Steel Heroes game data organized by Andy Aiken and the Forge Steel contributors. Names, keywords and ranges for the Elementalist were checked against the local Heroes v1.01b rules, including Practical Magic and Muse of Fire. The catalog is distributed under GPL-3.0. Its editable JSON is the preferred form for modifications here.
