# Texture sources

The Bathhouse material pass uses these Poly Haven texture sets, downloaded October 14, 2026:

- [Forest Ground 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/forrest_ground_01) — ground color, normal and roughness.
- [Rock Boulder Dry](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry) — rock and cliff color, normal and roughness.
- [Wood Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor) — wood color, normal and roughness.
- [Monastery Stone Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/monastery_stone_floor) — masonry and paving color, normal and roughness.

All are published under **CC0 1.0**. [Poly Haven license](https://polyhaven.com/license) · [CC0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

These are standalone material images, not extracted Dungeon Alchemist textures. Files are stored locally so opening the demo makes no requests to Poly Haven. The twelve maps total 8,842,636 download bytes. Exact source URLs, file hashes and byte counts are preserved in `manifest.json`. Download integrity was checked against the provider's published checksums.
