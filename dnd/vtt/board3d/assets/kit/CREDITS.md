# Art kit sources

All files here are CC0 1.0 (public domain), downloaded October 14, 2026 with Brandon's approval, and repacked
for the 3D board by the sandbox's `claude-art/build-kit.mjs` (models) and `build-textures.py` (WebP textures).

- `kit.json`, `kit.bin`, and `textures/kit-*.webp`: from **Quaternius, Stylized Nature MegaKit (Standard)**,
  CC0 1.0 (`stylized_nature_megakitstandard.zip`, opengameart.org). 26 of its glTF models: trees,
  twisted trees, fern, plants, clover, grass, flowers, bush, mushroom, rocks and pebbles.
- `textures/bark-*`, `moss-*`, `cliff-*`, `grass-*`: **Poly Haven** 1K texture sets `bark_willow`, `mossy_rock`,
  `rock_face_03` and `leafy_grass`, CC0 1.0 ([polyhaven.com/license](https://polyhaven.com/license)).
  The download checksums matched Poly Haven's published ones.
